Questions.

How accurate is BootFit?

Honest answer: it depends on the evidence. Recommendations built from a boot you already own are the most reliable. Every result carries a confidence tier, and when the evidence is thin we say so instead of guessing. As players log real purchase outcomes, accuracy improves for everyone.

How is this different from a size chart?

A size chart maps your foot to a number. BootFit maps one boot to another: it knows the Ultra 5 runs long, the Predator runs slightly short, and the F50 is too narrow to fix by sizing up. Charts cannot tell you any of that.

Why do you ask about my current boots?

Because a boot you wear every week is the most precise measuring device you own. How it fits in length and width tells us your foot shape better than any self-assessment about arches or toe types.

What if I do not own football boots?

Use Foot Profile mode: two measurements, your everyday sneakers, and three quick questions. It is clearly labeled as lower confidence, and the moment you add a boot that fits, your recommendations upgrade automatically.

Is my data private?

Fit reports are anonymous and only ever used in aggregate to improve sizing recommendations. We never sell personal data.

How the engine works

1. Your reference boot + size converts to a foot-length estimate in millimetres via the brand's size chart, adjusted by how you say it fits. 2. Each boot's known length tendency (runs short / true / long, in mm) shifts its effective internal length. 3. We pick the half-size closest to your preferred toe allowance (7 mm standard, 4 mm match fit). 4. Width is checked separately - a half size can't fix a narrow last, so mismatches produce a "consider another model" verdict instead of a bigger number. 5. Confidence reflects data quality and how cleanly your foot fits the target - it is never inflated to look precise.
